## 📌 Overview

**Lost & Found DApp** is a decentralized application designed to provide a transparent and reliable platform for managing lost and found items.

The application aims to connect people who have **lost belongings** with people who have **found them**, while exploring how blockchain technology can be applied to a practical real-world problem.

Instead of depending entirely on a centralized lost-and-found system, the project explores a decentralized approach where important records and interactions can be managed through blockchain-based technology.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Finding a lost item can be difficult when there is no centralized platform connecting the owner and the person who found it.

Traditional approaches often depend on:

* 📢 Social media posts
* 💬 Messaging groups
* 📋 Manual records
* 🏢 Physical lost-and-found offices
* 🔍 Unstructured searches

This makes the process difficult to track and can reduce the chances of successfully recovering an item.

### 💡 Our Approach

Lost & Found DApp provides a digital platform where users can:

```text
Report Lost Item
       ↓
Publish Item Details
       ↓
Browse / Search Records
       ↓
Identify Possible Match
       ↓
Verify Ownership
       ↓
Recover Lost Item
```

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🔍 Lost Item Reporting

Users can provide information about belongings that they have lost, making the information available for discovery.

### 📦 Found Item Reporting

Users who discover an item can publish its relevant details so that the rightful owner can search for it.

### 🔎 Item Discovery

Users can browse available records and look for items matching their lost belongings.

### 🔐 Decentralized Records

The project explores blockchain-based storage and transactions to improve transparency and traceability.

### 🤝 Community Participation

The platform encourages users to contribute information about lost and found belongings.

### ⛓️ Blockchain Integration

The project demonstrates how decentralized technologies can be applied to a real-world service instead of using blockchain only for financial applications.

## 🔄 User Journey

### 1️⃣ Report

A user reports a lost or found item.

### 2️⃣ Record

The relevant item information is recorded by the application.

### 3️⃣ Discover

Other users can browse available lost and found records.

### 4️⃣ Match

A possible match between a lost item and a found item can be identified.

Ownership can be verified before the item is returned.

### 6️⃣ Recover

The item can finally be returned to its rightful owner.
